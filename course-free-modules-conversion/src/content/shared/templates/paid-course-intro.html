HOW DO I USE THIS COURSE?

The 5-12 Month Sleep Course is fundamentally divided into two sections:

PART 1: The Prep
THE PREP will help you create an optimal environment for your baby to sleep, teach you about creating suitable routines, establish a feeding schedule and address common challenges that exist around baby sleep before we get to The Plan.

PART 2: The Plan
THE PLAN is a 14 day commitment that will transform your baby’s sleep patterns for the better. It takes the knowledge you gain in The Prep section and puts it into achievable, structured action.

The Prep and The Plan work together. For best results, I encourage you to read this course chronologically and in full.
