If you’ve found this Free Module, I’m guessing you’re in the thick of it. Your baby is waking constantly overnight, and you can not tell if they’re genuinely hungry or just looking for comfort.

You’re exhausted. You’re second-guessing yourself at 2am. And you’re wondering: should I feed them again, or am I creating a habit?

Here’s the truth: both things can be true. Some wakes are genuine hunger. Others are habit. The key is learning to tell the difference.

In this Free Module, I’m going to teach you exactly how to work out what’s hunger and what’s habit, then respond consistently so your baby learns when it’s feed time and when it’s resettle time.

You’ll learn my general rule: 3-hour intervals at 3 months, 4-hour intervals at 4 months. Plus practical strategies like the pre-bed feed technique and how to use a dreamfeed.

How to use this module

  1. Watch the video: I’ll walk you through the feed vs resettle decision and the timing guidelines.
  2. Download the guides: The Feed vs Resettle PDF helps you identify hunger vs habit signs. The Dreamfeed guide gives you step-by-step instructions if you want to try it.
  3. Apply tonight: Pick your intervals and commit to the approach for at least 3-4 nights before adjusting.
