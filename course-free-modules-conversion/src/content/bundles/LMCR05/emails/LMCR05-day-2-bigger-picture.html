I wanted to share a quick thought. Often, parents start this module thinking their baby is hungry every 2 hours. Then they realise that even after offering "full feeds" during the day, the waking continues.

That's usually where the "how" of sleep comes in. If your baby falls asleep feeding at bedtime, they naturally expect the same service when they wake at 2am.

We call these sleep associations. Feeding is a beautiful way to bond, but when it becomes the only way they can sleep, it's exhausting.
