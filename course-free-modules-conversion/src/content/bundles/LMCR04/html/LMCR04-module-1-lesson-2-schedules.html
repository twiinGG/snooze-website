How to use this module

  1. Focus on the one thing: Dive into the lessons below and implement the strategy for your baby’s specific age window.
  2. Observe the change: You should start to see a shift in your baby’s sleep as you apply these specific steps and anchor your feeds.
  3. Think bigger (when you’re ready): While this module solves a specific problem, sleep works best when it’s part of a complete, holistic plan.
