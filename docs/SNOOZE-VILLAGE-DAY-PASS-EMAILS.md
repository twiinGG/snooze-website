## Email 1: Post-Purchase Welcome Email

**Trigger:** Immediately after day pass is claimed  
**Purpose:** Welcome user, provide access instructions, set expectations

- `Welcome to Your Snooze Village Day Pass! 🌟`
- `Your 24-Hour Snooze Village Access Starts Now ✨`
- `Welcome! Your Day Pass is Ready 🎉`

### Email Body

```
Hi [First Name],

Welcome to your Snooze Village Day Pass! We're so excited to have you experience our community for the next 24 hours.

**What you can do right now:**
• Join Snooze Village and introduce yourself
• Attend our next live session (check the schedule below!)
• Ask questions and get support from our community
• Experience the warmth and encouragement that makes Snooze special

**How to access:**
1. Go to: https://joinsnooze.com/products/communities/v2/snooze
2. Log in with your account (the one you just created)
3. Start exploring!

**Upcoming Live Sessions:**
[Insert next scheduled live session date/time]
[Link to session schedule]

**Your 24-hour access starts now. Make the most of it!**

Questions? Just reply to this email - we're here to help.

With warmth,
The Snooze Team

P.S. Want full access to courses and the Library? Upgrade to a Snooze Membership anytime during your day pass!
```

### Kajabi Setup
- **Email Type:** Post-Purchase Email
- **Trigger:** Offer purchase completed
- **Delay:** 0 minutes (send immediately)
- **Personalization:** Use `[First Name]` and `[Product Name]` tags

## Email Design Guidelines

### Brand Colors
- **Primary (Coral):** `#F43357`
- **Navy:** `#1F293B`
- **Cream:** `#FAF7F4`
- **Sage:** `#7C8A98`

### Tone
- Warm and welcoming
- Non-judgmental
- Supportive
- Encouraging but not pushy

### Best Practices
- Use emojis sparingly (1-2 per email)
- Keep paragraphs short (2-3 sentences)
- Use bullet points for lists
- Include clear CTAs
- Personalize with first name
- Make links obvious and clickable
