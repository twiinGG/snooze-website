# Intro and Summary for Settling Techniques PDF

## Introduction

You're about to learn two proven settling techniques that have helped thousands of families move from bedtime battles and night wakings to calm, predictable sleep.

These techniques—**Soothe and Support Checks** and **The Vanishing Chair**—are taken directly from our 5-12 Month Sleep Course, where they're part of a complete system that addresses schedules, feeds, naps, and overnight settling. Here, we're giving you the settling piece so you can start making changes tonight.

Both techniques are structured, responsive, and supportive. They're designed to help your baby learn to fall asleep independently while you stay connected and present. The right choice for your family depends on your comfort level and your baby's temperament—and you might be surprised which one feels like the better fit.

**Soothe and Support Checks** works well if you're ready to step out of the room and use timed check-ins. It's clear, consistent, and gives your baby space to practice while you offer brief reassurance at set intervals.

**The Vanishing Chair** is perfect if you prefer to stay in the room at first. You gradually move your presence away from the cot over several nights, supporting your baby as they learn to settle with less hands-on help.

Whichever technique you choose, remember: consistency is everything. These methods work because they create a predictable pattern your baby can learn and trust. The first few nights might feel challenging, but that's normal. Your baby is learning a new skill, and like any skill, it takes practice.

You've got this. Let's dive in.
