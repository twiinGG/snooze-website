### Concrete, actionable fixes to reduce friction + anxiety

1. **Add “What happens next” microcopy under every primary CTA** (hero + mid-page + pricing). Example: “Choose plan → Create account → Instant access to sleep support.”
2. **Bring your strongest trust signals above the fold**: star rating + “Trusted by X parents” + one short testimonial with a real name/photo (if you have consent).
3. **Make the video earn its space**: rename it with a promise (“How Snooze helps you get longer stretches in 7 days”) and reduce its height so it doesn’t hijack the journey.
4. **Clarify risk reversal near pricing**: cancellation policy, guarantee (if offered), and “secure checkout” cues directly beside/under the pricing cards.
5. **Unify CTA language**: if it’s “Join Snooze,” keep that wording everywhere (avoid mixing “Join,” “Get started,” “Choose plan,” etc. unless each has a distinct step).
6. **Add navigation jump links** near the top: “What you get / Who it’s for / Reviews / Pricing / FAQ” to reduce scrolling fatigue.
7. **Reposition the “proof + outcomes” stack**: Hero (outcome) → bullets → proof → how it works. Right now the transition feels a little “hero → big brand video” instead of “hero → reassurance → next step.”
