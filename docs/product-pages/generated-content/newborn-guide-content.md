This guide is your companion through the fourth trimester—those first twelve weeks when everything feels new and you're learning your baby as they adjust to life outside the womb. You'll find practical, evidence-based strategies for understanding newborn sleep patterns, recognising when your baby needs rest, and creating gentle foundations that support sleep without rigid rules.
