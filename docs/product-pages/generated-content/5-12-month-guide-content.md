This course walks you through everything you need to know about your baby's sleep from 5-12 months—a time when sleep often feels unpredictable but is actually full of opportunity. You'll find clear, evidence-based strategies for both night sleep and naps, along with practical tools to help you create a plan that fits your family. Whether you're dealing with frequent night wakings, short naps, or simply want to feel more confident about your approach, this course gives you the structure and flexibility to move forward.
