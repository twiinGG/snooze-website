## 🎨 Design Changes

### Color Scheme (Dark Snooze Theme)
- **Background:** Dark Navy (#1F293B)
- **Text:** White (#FFFFFF) on dark sections
- **Accent:** Coral/Pink (#F43357) for buttons and highlights
- **Content Panels:** White backgrounds for light sections
- **Muted Text:** Light gray (#94A3B8) for secondary text

### Structure Changes

**From Sales-Focused:**
- ❌ Large callout banners
- ❌ Multiple comparison tables
- ❌ Testimonial carousels
- ❌ "How It Works" sales process
- ❌ Multiple CTAs competing for attention

**To Resource-Focused:**
- ✅ Simplified hero with course stats
- ✅ Curriculum section (prominent, expandable)
- ✅ Course resources list (tangible items)
- ✅ "How to Use This Course" (practical guidance)
- ✅ Single pricing card (clean, simple)
- ✅ Educational tone throughout
