# Product Page Redesign Proposal: From Sales Page to Course Resource

**Date:** December 06, 2025  
**Focus:** 3-4 Month Course Landing Page  
**Goal:** Transform product pages from sales-focused to resource-focused, making them feel like course previews rather than marketing pages

---

## Current Problem Analysis

### What's Too Sales-Heavy

1. **Hero Section** - Large, marketing-focused with "FOR PARENTS WHO WANT SLEEP WITHOUT STRESS" callout
2. **Multiple Comparison Sections** - Value comparison appears 3 times (individual product value, membership upgrade, detailed comparison)
3. **Testimonials Section** - Feels like social proof marketing
4. **Instructor Section** - "Meet Your Instructor" feels like course platform marketing
5. **Multiple CTAs** - Purchase buttons appear 4+ times throughout
6. **"How It Works"** - Process steps feel like sales funnel
7. **Problem/Solution Framing** - "Why This Age is Different" feels like marketing copy

### What's Missing (Course Resource Feel)

1. **Course Content Preview** - Curriculum is buried, should be prominent
2. **Learning Outcomes** - What will parents actually learn/achieve?
3. **Course Structure** - How is the course organized? What's the learning path?
4. **Resource Types** - What formats are included? (videos, PDFs, checklists, etc.)
5. **Time Investment** - How long does the course take? How is it paced?
6. **Practical Application** - How do parents use this in real life?

---

## Proposed Design: Resource-First Approach

### Core Philosophy Shift

**From:** "Buy this course to solve your problem"  
**To:** "Here's what's inside this course - preview the content and see if it's right for you"

**Design Principle:** Lead with value and content, make purchase feel like natural next step, not primary goal.

---

## New Page Structure

### 1. **Simplified Hero** (Less Sales, More Course Preview)

**Current:** Large hero with callout banner, multiple benefit bullets, trust signal, CTA

**Proposed:**
- Course title and subtitle (clear, educational)
- Brief course description (what it is, not why you need it)
- Quick course stats (modules, lessons, resources)
- Subtle CTA (not primary focus)

**Example:**
```
3-4 Month Baby Sleep Course

A self-paced course covering sleep changes, nap consolidation, and building independent sleep skills for babies 3-4 months old.

11 modules • 20+ lessons • Downloadable resources • On-demand access
```

**Design Notes:**
- Remove callout banner
- Remove trust signal (move to footer or remove)
- Smaller hero, more content-focused
- CTA is secondary, not hero-sized

---

### 2. **Course Overview** (NEW - Lead Section)

**Purpose:** Immediately show what the course contains

**Content:**
- What you'll learn (learning outcomes, not benefits)
- Course format (text-based, videos, PDFs, etc.)
- Time investment (self-paced, estimated hours)
- Prerequisites (none, or "recommended for babies 3-4 months")

**Design:**
- Clean, educational layout
- Bullet points or simple grid
- No icons or marketing graphics
- Feels like a course syllabus preview

---

### 3. **Course Curriculum** (PROMINENT - Move Up)

**Current:** Buried in middle of page, after sales content

**Proposed:** Move to top third of page, expand with more detail

**Enhanced Content:**
- Module-by-module breakdown
- Lesson titles (not just module names)
- Learning objectives per module
- Resource types per module (video, PDF, checklist, etc.)
- Estimated time per module

**Design:**
- Accordion or expandable sections
- Clean typography, easy to scan
- Feels like a course catalog or syllabus
- Can expand to see full lesson list

**Example Structure:**
```
Module 1: Understanding 3-4 Month Sleep Changes
├─ Lesson 1: Sleep Cycle Maturation (5 min)
├─ Lesson 2: Why Cat Naps Happen (8 min)
├─ Lesson 3: Night Wake Patterns (6 min)
└─ Resource: Sleep Cycle Diagram (PDF)

Module 2: Creating Optimal Sleep Environment
├─ Lesson 1: Room Setup Checklist (10 min)
├─ Lesson 2: Blackout & White Noise (7 min)
└─ Resource: Environment Checklist (PDF)
```

---

### 4. **What's Included** (Resource-Focused)

**Current:** "What You'll Learn Inside" with feature icons

**Proposed:** "Course Resources" - tangible items included

**Content:**
- Video lessons (X videos, Y total minutes)
- Written guides (X guides, downloadable PDFs)
- Checklists and trackers (X printable resources)
- Sample schedules (X age-appropriate schedules)
- Flowcharts and decision trees (X visual guides)

**Design:**
- Simple list or grid
- Resource count, not marketing copy
- Feels like a course materials list
- No "benefits" language, just facts

---

### 5. **How to Use This Course** (Practical, Not Sales)

**Current:** "How It Works" with numbered steps (Buy → Watch → Implement → See Results)

**Proposed:** "How to Use This Course" - practical guidance

**Content:**
- Self-paced learning approach
- Recommended order (sequential vs. jump around)
- How to track progress
- When to implement strategies
- How to reference back to content

**Design:**
- Educational tone
- Practical tips, not sales process
- Feels like course instructions
- No "Buy Once" messaging

---

### 6. **Is This Course Right for You?** (Replace "Target Audience")

**Current:** "This Course is for You If..." with checkmarks

**Proposed:** "Is This Course Right for You?" - helps parents self-assess

**Content:**
- Who this course is designed for (age range, challenges)
- What you'll need (time, commitment level)
- When this course is most helpful
- Alternative options if this isn't right (link to other courses/ages)

**Design:**
- Educational, not marketing
- Helps parents decide, doesn't sell
- Can include "Not sure? Try this instead" links

---

### 7. **Course Access & Pricing** (Simplified, Less Prominent)

**Current:** Multiple pricing sections, comparison tables, membership upsells

**Proposed:** Single, simple pricing section

**Content:**
- Individual course price ($117)
- What's included (on-demand access, updates, etc.)
- Purchase button (clear but not oversized)
- Optional: "Also available in Snooze Membership" (subtle link, not full section)

**Design:**
- Clean pricing card
- No comparison tables on main page
- Membership mention is subtle, not competing
- Feels like course enrollment, not sales pitch

---

### 8. **About the Course Creator** (Replace "Meet Your Instructor")

**Current:** "Meet Your Instructor" with large photo and credentials

**Proposed:** "About the Course Creator" - brief, factual

**Content:**
- Sally's credentials (brief)
- Why she created this course
- Her approach/philosophy (brief)
- Link to full bio if needed

**Design:**
- Smaller, less prominent
- Text-focused, not image-heavy
- Feels like course credits, not marketing
- Can be at bottom or sidebar

---

### 9. **FAQ** (Keep, But Make More Educational)

**Current:** Sales-focused FAQs (pricing, results, updates)

**Proposed:** Course-focused FAQs

**Content:**
- Course structure questions
- How to access content
- Technical questions
- Content updates
- Practical implementation questions

**Design:**
- Educational tone
- Helps parents understand the course
- Not sales objections

---

### 10. **Related Resources** (NEW)

**Purpose:** Connect to other content, feels like resource library

**Content:**
- Related age guides
- Related troubleshooting resources
- Link to Snooze Method (foundational course)
- Link to Library (if member)

**Design:**
- Simple link list or cards
- Feels like course library navigation
- Not sales-focused

## Visual Design Changes

### Typography & Layout
- **More white space** - Less dense, easier to read
- **Larger body text** - Feels like reading course content
- **Clearer hierarchy** - Course content is most prominent
- **Less color/bold** - More subtle, professional
- **Educational fonts** - Less marketing, more academic

### Components to Remove/Reduce
- ❌ Large hero CTAs
- ❌ Multiple comparison tables
- ❌ Testimonial carousels
- ❌ "How It Works" process graphics
- ❌ Large instructor photos
- ❌ Sticky CTA bars (or make subtle)

### Components to Add/Enhance
- ✅ Course curriculum accordion
- ✅ Resource list/details
- ✅ Learning outcomes
- ✅ Course structure diagram
- ✅ Module previews
- ✅ Time estimates

---

## Content Tone Shift

### From Sales Language:
- "Beat the 4-month regression"
- "See results in days"
- "Trusted by thousands"
- "Best value"
- "Join now"

### To Resource Language:
- "Covers sleep changes at 3-4 months"
- "Self-paced learning"
- "Created by former paediatric nurse"
- "Includes X resources"
- "Access the course"

---

## Purchase Flow Integration

### Primary Purchase Option
- Individual course purchase ($117)
- Clear, simple button
- Not competing with other options on main page

### Membership Option
- Subtle mention: "This course is also included in Snooze Membership"
- Link to membership page or checkout
- Not full comparison section
- Can be in sidebar or footer

### Context-Aware Elements
- Members see "Access in Library" instead of purchase
- Logged-in non-members see "Upgrade to Snooze" option
- New visitors see purchase option
