### The Camp Snooze Case Study: 6 Families, 14 Days

Camp Snooze is a premium cohort-based program run by Sally Woods and Bec Maher (The Sleep Centre). The January 2026 cohort tracked 6 families across Australia (VIC, NSW, QLD) over 14 days, producing the most detailed outcome data Snooze has compiled to date.

**The cohort included six babies, each with a distinct sleep challenge:**

| Baby | Age | Archetype | Starting Issues |
|------|-----|-----------|----------------|
| W. | 10.5 months | The Busy Boy | Reflux history, car naps, frequent spit-ups, heavily reliant on dummy |

**Baseline:** Every family was in survival mode. The average was 4 to 5 night wakes per family. 100% of babies relied on parental assistance (rocking, feeding, patting) to fall asleep.

### The Methodology: Three Pillars

Camp Snooze is built on three pillars that mirror what works in any behaviour-change program:

**The Plan:** Tailored, age-appropriate schedules. For older babies (W., M.), this meant transitioning from 3 naps to 2 to increase sleep pressure and consolidate overnight sleep.

**The Techniques:** A menu of settling methods matched to each family's comfort level:
- The Vanishing Chair: gradual parental withdrawal (used for R.)
- Spaced Soothing: timed intervals of checking in (used for A.)
- Cot Time: dedicating 1.5 hours to the cot for Nap 2 to build sleep pressure

**The Village:** Daily group coaching calls and a forum where families could share the struggle and the wins. This community element was not a bonus feature. It was a core mechanism.

## Section 2: Strategic Fit (Snooze x Kic)

### Audience Overlap

**Kic's core user:** Women aged 25 to 40 in Australia, digitally native, interested in holistic wellness (movement, mindset, nutrition, community). 2.5 million+ community members, 750,000+ app downloads, users in 121+ countries.

**Snooze's core user:** Tired parents with baby sleep challenges, primarily Australia initially. High disposable income, value expert guidance, active on social media, willing to invest in family wellness.

**The overlap:** A significant portion of Kic's audience is moving through fertility, pregnancy, and early parenthood within the lifetime of their membership. Kic already explicitly serves this audience through KICBump (pre/postnatal Pilates), a postnatal running program, and the KICBump podcast described as "your podcast from birth, to baby, and beyond."

The gap: when these women become mothers and their biggest barrier to movement, mindset, and nutrition is a baby who won't sleep, Kic currently has no specialist answer. Their sleep content is geared to adult wind-down rituals and meditations, not family sleep routines.

### Values Alignment

| Value | Kic | Snooze |
|-------|-----|--------|
| Evidence-based | Behavioural change frameworks, expert-reviewed programs | Registered nurse credentials, evidence-based methodology |
| Anti-perfection | "Wellness is a feeling, not an aesthetic" | "Progress, not perfection. First night might look messy." |
| Sustainable habits | "Rituals for real life," habit formation | "The skills don't change just because the location does" |
| Community-first | "Stronger together," Facebook groups, IRL events | "The Village" as a core pillar, group coaching model |
| No judgment | "Inclusivity, authenticity, kindness" | "There is no right or wrong. If it works for you, fantastic." |
| Holistic approach | Movement, mindset, rituals, nutrition | "Looking at sleep through a holistic lens" |

This is not superficial alignment. Both brands reject diet-culture/perfection framing. Both prioritise how people feel over how things look. Both build communities that support behaviour change, not just content consumption.

### Product Fit: Sleep as a Kic Pillar

Sleep is already inside Kic's product architecture:

- Kic's "Rituals" page includes 50+ wind-down Pilates classes, 100+ yoga/mobility flows, and 100+ sleep-inducing meditations
- The January 2026 app refresh named "sleep, confidence, focus and calm" as new strategic pillars
- The Sheet Society "Rest Club" partnership explicitly framed sleep as self-care
- Janey's podcast appearance (January 2026) discussed Emotional Freedom Techniques, hypnotherapy, and pathways including "calm, rest, and focus"

The opportunity: Kic owns adult sleep rituals. Nobody in the fitness/wellness app space owns "family sleep health" for parents of babies and toddlers. That is white space, and it is white space that directly affects Kic's core promise (sustainable wellness habits) because sleep-deprived parents cannot sustain exercise, nutrition, or mindset practices.

Research supports this: a 2025 Monash University study showed that earlier bedtimes and sleep timing are associated with next-day physical activity. A 2025 postpartum sleep study reported new mothers average 4.4 hours of daily sleep in the first postpartum week, with the longest uninterrupted stretch dropping to 2.2 hours.

### Janey Martino Alignment

Janey's background and public statements create unusually strong alignment with Snooze:

- **Smiling Mind co-founder and Chair:** 9.3 million downloads, 14 million young people reached. She has dedicated her career to accessible, evidence-based mental health tools. Baby sleep directly affects parental mental health and child emotional regulation.
- **Behavioural change expertise:** Kic's January 2026 refresh includes "values-based goal setting and reflection tools" linked to her background. Camp Snooze is fundamentally a behaviour-change program (new habits in 14 days).
- **Generational impact:** Janey consistently speaks about creating multi-generational impact. Improving family sleep affects parents, babies, and the quality of early attachment, making it one of the highest-leverage interventions in early childhood.
- **Mental health parity:** She argues that future generations should think about mental health with the same seriousness as diet and exercise. Sleep is the connective tissue between physical and mental health.
- **"Have a plan but stay open to a better one":** Her stated leadership philosophy pairs ambition with flexibility. A phased partnership approach (start small, grow based on data) matches this mindset.
- **Personal connection to maternal wellbeing:** Sally has openly shared her own experience with postnatal depression and a traumatic birth. This is not a talking point; it is lived experience that informs how she supports families. Janey's career has been built on making mental health tools accessible. A partnership rooted in supporting maternal mental health through better family sleep is one of the most authentic alignments either brand could pursue.

### The Business Case for Kic

**Retention lever:** Sleep-deprived new parents are at high risk of pausing or cancelling fitness subscriptions. If Kic actively helps them with their primary problem (baby sleep), it retains them through a critical life stage instead of losing them to churn.

**Differentiation:** No major fitness or wellness app owns "family sleep health." This gives Kic a unique pillar versus Sweat, Centr, Apple Fitness+, and every other competitor in the Australian and global market.

**ARPU uplift:** Co-branded sleep programs can be packaged as premium tracks or bundles, creating incremental revenue while delivering strong member value.

**Reactivation:** Former Kic members who paused after having a baby are a natural reactivation audience. Sleep support gives them a reason to come back.

### Market Precedents

This type of partnership is well-established in the wellness space:

- Nike Run Club partnered with Headspace on mindfulness-guided runs
- Fitbit partnered with Calm for premium member sleep and meditation offers
- Oura positioned sleep scientist Matthew Walker as a medical advisor
- Barry's partnered with Eight Sleep as "Official Sleep Fitness Partner"
- The American Council on Exercise launched a sleep coaching course with Nick Lambe

Sleep support is a normal, credible expansion for a fitness/wellness proposition. It is not a category jump.

## Section 3: Partnership Options

### Option 1: Content Expert / Sleep Module (Lowest Friction)

**What it is:** Sally provides a curated library of parent-focused sleep content for the Kic app, sitting within the Mindset or Rituals section. Short, modular video lessons (5 to 15 minutes) covering newborn, baby, and toddler sleep foundations.

**Content could include:**
- "Micro-rituals" for parents (3 to 10 minutes): bedtime routine scripts, wind-down sequences, partner coordination
- Age-specific sleep education modules
- "2am reset" audio talks for exhausted parents (complementing Kic's existing meditation library)
- Troubleshooting guides for common challenges (regressions, teething, travel)

**What it requires from Snooze:**
- Sally records content to Kic's production standards
- Content adapted to Kic's tone (already highly aligned)
- Ongoing content updates (quarterly or as new sleep stages are added)
- Sally available for 1 to 2 promotional appearances per quarter

**What it requires from Kic:**
- Section within the app (Mindset, Rituals, or dedicated "Family Sleep" hub)
- Production support (filming, editing to Kic brand standards)
- Marketing and distribution within the app and across Kic channels
- Attribution: "In partnership with Snooze by The Sleep Concierge"

**Pros:**
- Lowest barrier to entry for both sides
- Scalable (content created once, consumed by all members)
- Tests audience appetite before deeper integration
- Fits Kic's existing content model

**Cons:**
- Limited revenue upside for Snooze compared to deeper models
- No direct relationship between Sally and Kic members
- Risk of content feeling "bolted on" if not well-integrated

**Commercial structure:** Content licensing fee (flat or per-asset) + revenue share on any premium module upsell. Alternatively, a retainer model for ongoing content creation.

---

### Option 2: Co-Created Program ("Bump to Toddler" Sleep Pathway)

**What it is:** A structured, progressive program analogous to KICRun or KICBump, but focused on family sleep. A 4 to 6 week pathway that combines gentle Kic movement, mindset tools, and parallel baby-sleep micro-lessons by Sally.

**Program framing:** "From Survival to Sustainable" - helping parents transition from chaotic sleep to predictable routines, while slowly reintroducing movement and self-care.

**Structure could include:**
- Week 1 to 2: Understanding your baby's sleep (education + gentle movement for exhausted bodies)
- Week 3 to 4: Building the routine (sleep plan implementation + mindset tools for consistency)
- Week 5 to 6: Troubleshooting and confidence (advanced scenarios + rebuilding fitness habits)
- Daily touchpoints combining Kic content (movement, meditation) with Snooze content (sleep guidance)
- Community element: group forum or challenge board

**What it requires from Snooze:**
- Sally co-designs the sleep curriculum
- Records program content (video modules, audio guides)
- Participates in launch marketing
- Available for live Q&A sessions during program runs (1 to 2 per run)

**What it requires from Kic:**
- Program production and app integration
- Existing movement and mindset content curated for the program
- Marketing budget and channel promotion
- Community infrastructure for the program

**Pros:**
- High-value, differentiated product for Kic
- Deeper integration creates stronger brand association
- Program model allows for recurring cohorts (3 to 4 times per year)
- Community element mirrors what works in both brands

**Cons:**
- Higher investment of time and resources from both sides
- Longer lead time to launch (3 to 6 months development)
- Requires coordination on curriculum and brand standards

**Commercial structure:** Co-branded program with revenue share. Could be included for all Kic members (retention play) or offered as a premium add-on (ARPU play). Snooze receives a creation fee plus ongoing royalty or per-participant fee.

### Option 3: Cross-Promotion / Bundle (Quick Win)

**What it is:** A joint marketing play that shares audiences and creates bundled offers, without deep product integration. Launches fast, generates data, and builds the relationship.

**Tactics could include:**
- Joint lead magnet: "Sleep and Energy Reset for New Parents" (downloadable checklist combining Kic movement tips + Snooze sleep tips), driving email signups for both lists
- Discount code exchange: Kic members get a discount on Snooze membership; Snooze members get a discount on Kic annual subscription
- Co-branded challenge: "14 Nights of Wind-Down" combining journalling prompts, short movement, and family sleep scripts (mirrors Kic's existing challenge architecture)
- Joint social content: Sally appears on KICPod or KICBump podcast; Laura/Steph or Janey appear on Nap Trapped

**What it requires from Snooze:**
- Create co-branded lead magnet content
- Set up discount codes and tracking
- Promote Kic to Snooze audience
- Sally available for podcast/social appearances

**What it requires from Kic:**
- Promote Snooze to Kic audience via email, app, social
- Set up discount codes and tracking
- Host Sally on podcast/social channels
- Share performance data (downloads, conversions, engagement)

**Pros:**
- Fast to launch (2 to 4 weeks)
- Low investment, low risk
- Generates real audience data before deeper commitment
- Builds personal relationship between teams

**Cons:**
- Limited revenue impact compared to deeper integration
- Risk of feeling transactional if not followed by deeper partnership
- Discount codes alone do not create lasting brand association

**Commercial structure:** Performance-based (track conversions from each side). No upfront cost. Revenue from new members attributed to the partnership. Kic has an existing affiliate program via Commission Factory with published commission and cookie length, providing a clean performance-based pilot mechanism.

### Option 4: Ambassador / Expert Role (High Trust, High Visibility)

**What it is:** Sally becomes Kic's resident sleep expert, appearing regularly across Kic's channels. This is a credibility play for both brands: Kic gains a clinical authority in family sleep, and Sally gains distribution to 2.5 million+ community members.

**Activities could include:**
- Recurring guest on KICPod and KICBump podcast
- Monthly live Q&A sessions for Kic members ("Ask Sally" format)
- Featured expert in app content (Mindset or Rituals section)
- Representation at Kic IRL events and activations
- Contributions to Kic editorial content (blog, email, social)
- "Office hours" format inside community channels

**What it requires from Snooze:**
- Sally commits a set number of hours per month (e.g. 8 to 12 hours)
- Content creation for Kic's channels
- Attendance at 1 to 2 IRL events per year
- Alignment on messaging and brand standards

**What it requires from Kic:**
- Ambassador contract with defined scope and compensation
- Channel access and promotion (podcast, app, email, social, events)
- Attribution: "Kic Sleep Expert, Sally Woods of Snooze by The Sleep Concierge"
- Collaboration on content calendar

**Pros:**
- Highest visibility for Sally and Snooze brand
- Builds deep, personal connection with Kic audience
- Creates ongoing content pipeline for both brands
- Strongest retention play (members associate Kic with having "their" sleep expert)

**Cons:**
- Highest time commitment from Sally
- Need to protect against exclusivity constraints that limit Snooze's growth
- Risk if the relationship does not work at a personal level

**Commercial structure:** Monthly retainer or quarterly fee for ambassador services. Performance bonuses tied to engagement metrics (attendance at live sessions, content views). Cross-promotion built into the agreement.

### Recommended Approach: Start With Option 3, Build to Option 1 + 4

We recommend a phased approach that matches Janey's stated philosophy ("have a plan but stay open to a better one"):

**Phase 1 (Month 1 to 2): Cross-Promotion Pilot (Option 3)**
- Joint lead magnet, podcast appearances, discount codes
- Generate audience data and test chemistry
- Low risk, fast execution

**Phase 2 (Month 3 to 6): Content Expert + Ambassador (Option 1 + 4)**
- Sally begins creating sleep content for the app
- Regular podcast and live appearances
- First "Rest Club for Parents" challenge or community event

**Phase 3 (Month 6 to 12): Co-Created Program (Option 2)**
- Based on data from Phases 1 and 2, co-design a structured program
- "Bump to Toddler" sleep pathway or "Parent Sleep Reset"
- Full integration into Kic's program architecture

This progression mirrors how Kic's other partnerships have developed: Sheet Society started with a "Rest Club" concept before deeper integration. New Balance has been a long-term partner "since 2023" with deepening collaboration over time.
