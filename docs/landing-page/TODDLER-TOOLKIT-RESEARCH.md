## 6. Key Methods and Approaches Extracted

### 6.1 Early Rising Fix Method

**From Video ID: 7409835060658064648:**
1. Treat 5am as overnight waking (not start of day)
2. Use low-stimulation resettling technique
3. Time checks every 10 minutes when escalated
4. Continue until 6:45-7am
5. When 7am arrives: lights, food, socialization
6. Push through to scheduled nap time (don't bring nap earlier)

### 6.2 Nap Transition Method

**From Video ID: 7430662164693749000:**
1. Use transitional schedule (short/long)
2. First nap: 10-10:30am, 15-30 minutes (can be assisted)
3. Second nap: 1-3pm (protected, long nap)
4. When first nap refused even when fully assisted → ready for 1 nap
5. Bring second nap earlier (11:30am initially)
6. Gradually move to 12:30pm over 2 weeks

### 6.3 Separation Anxiety Method

**From Video ID: 7282938224505392402:**
1. Slow down bedtime routine
2. Give toddler autonomy (choose jammies, books)
3. More connection moments in routine
4. 20 minutes one-on-one play daily
5. In-room approach: Sit on chair, gradually move further away
6. Camping out: Mattress on floor, gradually move away
7. Honor the anxiety while upholding boundaries

### 6.4 Bedtime Routine Method

**From Video ID: 7546175157581155592:**
1. Start from dinner (big lean into bedtime)
2. Bath early (5:30pm) - can overstimulate if too close to bed
3. Rough housing/sensory play (6pm)
4. Final feed 30 minutes before bed (out on lounge)
5. Into dark room at 6:45pm
6. Bed at 7pm
