## Course Landing Page Content

**Course Title:** The Snooze Method: Foundational Course

**Course Subtitle:** Core Philosophy, Mindset, and Framework for Better Baby Sleep

**Course Description:**
The Snooze Method Foundational Course is your starting point for understanding the core philosophy, mindset, and framework that underlies all Snooze guidance. This course fills the critical Tier 1 gap in your learning journey, establishing The Snooze Method as the foundational framework that makes all other Snooze content make sense.

Whether you're dealing with a newborn, navigating the 4-month sleep regression, or managing toddler sleep challenges, this course gives you the "why" behind all Snooze recommendations. You'll learn how to think about sleep challenges, when to take action versus when to wait, and how to adapt The Snooze Method to your unique family situation.

**Who It's For:**
- All new Snooze members, regardless of your baby's age or stage
- Parents who want to understand the "why" behind sleep guidance
- Families looking for a clear framework for thinking about sleep
- Anyone feeling overwhelmed by conflicting sleep advice
- Parents who want to build confidence in their sleep decisions

**What's Included:**
- 4 comprehensive modules
- 16 foundational lessons
- 4-6 downloadable resources (quick reference guides, checklists, decision frameworks)
- Links to relevant TikTok videos and Nap Trapped podcast episodes
- Real Q&A examples from Sally
- Step-by-step frameworks you can apply immediately

**Learning Outcomes:**
By the end of this course, you'll:
1. Understand what The Snooze Method is and how it's different from other approaches
2. Appreciate the evidence-based foundation that informs all Snooze guidance
3. Recognize the importance of parent confidence and how The Snooze Method builds it
4. Know how to balance structure with real-life flexibility
5. Be able to evaluate sleep advice critically (evidence-based vs. anecdotal)
6. Have a framework for thinking about sleep challenges
7. Know when to implement changes versus when to wait for developmental readiness
8. Understand sleep associations and how they work
9. Be able to distinguish developmental changes from behavioral issues
10. Know how to adapt The Snooze Method to your unique family situation
11. Understand when to seek additional support
12. Have clear next steps in your Snooze learning journey

**Course Format:**
- Text-based lessons (launch version)
- Embedded TikTok videos
- Links to relevant podcast episodes with timestamps
- Q&A example boxes
- Downloadable resources
- Callout boxes for key takeaways

**Time to Complete:**
- Estimated 2-3 hours total
- Can be completed in one sitting or spread over several days
- Designed to be your first step before age-based modules

**Prerequisites:**
None. This is the starting point for all Snooze members.

**Next Steps After Completion:**
After completing this foundational course, you'll be ready to:
- Move to age-based modules (Newborn, 3-4 Month Course, 5-12 Month Guide, Toddler Toolkit)
- Use sleep troubleshooting resources with confidence
- Apply The Snooze Method framework to your specific situation
- Participate in Program channels with foundational understanding
