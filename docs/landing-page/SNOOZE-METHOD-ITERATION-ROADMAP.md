**Tracking Methods:**
- Learning path progression (Tier 1 → Tier 2 → Tier 3)
- Support channel question analysis
- Post-completion surveys
- Member feedback in Program channels

**Target Metrics:**

**Tracking Methods:**
- Post-completion survey

## Feedback Collection Methods

### 1. Post-Completion Survey

**Timing:** Immediately after course completion  
**Questions:**
1. What was most helpful about this course? (open text)
2. What was confusing or unclear? (open text)
3. What topics need more depth? (open text)
4. What's missing that you expected to see? (open text)
5. How confident do you feel applying The Snooze Method? (1-5 scale)
6. How clear was the content? (1-5 scale)
7. How relevant was the content to your situation? (1-5 scale)
8. Would you recommend this course to other members? (yes/no)
9. What would you change or improve? (open text)

**Distribution:**
- Automated email after completion
- Kajabi survey integration
- Optional in-course survey

### 2. Community Channel Discussions

**Where:** Program channels, Snooze Village  
**Questions to Ask:**
- "For those who've completed The Snooze Method course, what was most valuable?"
- "What questions do you still have after completing the foundational course?"
- "What topics would you like to see expanded?"

**Tracking:**
- Monitor discussions for themes
- Document common questions
- Identify content gaps

### 3. Support Team Observations

**What to Track:**
- Questions answered by course content
- Questions that indicate course gaps
- Member confusion points
- Common misunderstandings

**Reporting:**
- Weekly summary of support observations
- Monthly analysis of patterns
- Quarterly review of course effectiveness

### 4. Learning Analytics

**Kajabi Analytics to Monitor:**
- Course completion rates
- Time spent per lesson
- Drop-off points
- Return visits
- Most viewed lessons
- Least viewed lessons

**Analysis:**
- Identify lessons that need revision
- Find content that's most valuable
- Understand learning patterns
- Optimize course flow

## Iteration Priorities

### Priority 1: Content Gaps (Based on Feedback)

**Potential Additions:**
- **Module 5: The Science of Sleep** (deeper dive into sleep science)
  - Sleep cycles explained in detail
  - Sleep pressure and wake windows science
  - Circadian rhythm basics
  - Sleep associations and habits (deeper)

- **Module 6: Common Misconceptions and Myth-Busting**
  - "No tears" methods reality check
  - "Gentle" vs. evidence-based distinction
  - Sleep training myths debunked
  - What research actually says

- **Module 7: Building Long-Term Sleep Habits**
  - Maintaining progress over time
  - Handling regressions with confidence
  - Adapting as baby grows
  - Building sustainable routines

- **Module 8: The Snooze Method for Different Parenting Styles**
  - Attachment-focused adaptations
  - Structured approach adaptations
  - Finding your balance
  - Respecting different values

**Decision Criteria:**
- 20%+ of members request specific topic
- Support team identifies recurring gap
- Learning analytics show confusion point
- Strategic alignment with category leadership

### Priority 2: Clarity Improvements

**Areas to Monitor:**
- Lessons with high drop-off rates
- Support questions about specific lessons
- Survey feedback on unclear content
- Member-reported confusion points

**Improvement Types:**
- Add more examples
- Simplify explanations
- Add visual aids (diagrams, flowcharts)
- Break complex concepts into smaller pieces
- Add more Q&A examples

### Priority 3: Depth Expansion

**High-Value Topics for Expansion:**
- Evidence-based approach (more research examples)
- Parent confidence framework (more strategies)
- Decision-making frameworks (more scenarios)
- Method adaptation (more examples)

**Expansion Format:**
- Additional lessons in existing modules
- Expanded explanations in current lessons
- Downloadable resources (decision trees, frameworks)
- Video explanations for complex concepts

### Priority 4: Format Enhancements

**Potential Enhancements:**
- **Video Production:** High-production value videos for key concepts
- **Interactive Elements:** Quizzes, reflection prompts, worksheets
- **Downloadable Resources:** Quick reference guides, decision frameworks, checklists
- **Progress Tracking:** Visual progress indicators, completion certificates

**Decision Criteria:**
- Member requests for specific formats
- Engagement data shows format preference
- Strategic value for category leadership
- Resource availability
