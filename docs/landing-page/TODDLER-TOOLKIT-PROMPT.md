### Brand Voice Principles (CRITICAL - Apply Throughout)
1. **Evidence-Based Clarity:** Research-backed information in plain language, never academic jargon
2. **Supportive Without Coddling:** Acknowledges difficulty while maintaining realistic expectations
3. **Practical and Actionable:** Every piece of advice includes specific, implementable steps
4. **Confident and Calm:** Reduces parent anxiety through certainty and expertise
5. **Judgment-Free:** Never shames parents for choices; validates different paths
6. **Relatable and Human:** Shares personal experiences, admits challenges, shows vulnerability

### Language Guidelines
**DO USE:**
- "I would" / "I like to" / "For me" (personal expertise)
- "You can" / "You don't have to" (permission-giving)
- "Usually" / "Often" / "For most toddlers" (realistic)
- "This is not because you've done anything wrong" (removes shame)
- "I know how hard this is" (validates)
- Specific timings and schedules
- Step-by-step processes with "why" explained

**DON'T USE:**
- "You should" / "You must" (judgmental)
- "Always" / "Never" (too absolute)
- "Perfect" / "Flawless" (unrealistic)
- "Gentle vs harsh" (method war language)
- "Failure" / "Broken" (shaming)
- Vague advice without specifics

And finally, if bedtime has become a power struggle, it can become a habit that's hard to break.
>
