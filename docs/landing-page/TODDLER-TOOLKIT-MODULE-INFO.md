## Course Landing Page Content

**Course Title:** Toddler Toolkit

**Course Description:**
A comprehensive collection of tools, strategies, and resources for managing toddler sleep challenges, transitions, and bedtime routines. This self-guided toolkit assumes you're already familiar with the Snooze Method foundations from the 5-12 Month Sleep Guide or 3-4 Month Sleep Course.

**Who It's For:**
Parents and caregivers of toddlers aged 12+ months dealing with:
- Bedtime battles and resistance
- Nap transitions (2-to-1, dropping naps)
- Early rising
- Night wakings
- Sleep regressions
- Transitioning from crib to bed
- Travel and routine disruptions
- Separation anxiety at bedtime
- Sleep associations and dependencies

**What's Included:**
- 7 comprehensive modules
- 25 practical lessons
- 15+ downloadable resources (schedules, checklists, scripts, troubleshooting guides)
- Links to relevant TikTok videos and Nap Trapped podcast episodes
- Step-by-step strategies you can implement immediately

**Learning Outcomes:**
By the end of this toolkit, you'll:
1. Understand common toddler sleep challenges and why they happen
2. Have age-appropriate schedules and routines for your toddler
3. Know how to handle nap transitions confidently
4. Have practical tools for bedtime battles and resistance
5. Understand how to manage early rising in toddlers
6. Know how to handle sleep regressions and developmental changes
7. Have scripts and phrases for difficult moments
8. Feel confident implementing changes and troubleshooting issues
