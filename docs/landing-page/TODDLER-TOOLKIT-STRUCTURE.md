- Video: 7304200428877663496 - "Example bedtime routine"
- 10+ Q&A pairs on bedtime struggles

**Key Bedtime Routine Documented:**
- 5pm: Dinner (solids, family meal)
- 5:30pm: Bath
- 6pm: Rough housing/sensory play
- 6:30pm: Final milk feed (out on lounge, 30 min before bed)
- 6:45pm: Into dark room, white noise, sleeping bag, story/songs
- 7pm: Into bed

**Key Strategies Documented:**
- Slow down bedtime routine
- Give toddler autonomy (choose jammies, books)
- More connection moments in routine
- 20 minutes one-on-one play daily
- In-room approach: Sit on chair, gradually move further away
- Camping out: Mattress on floor, gradually move away

- Video: 7268048201721089281 - "Night terrors"

**Key Early Rising Method Documented:**
1. Treat 5am as overnight waking (not start of day)
2. Use low-stimulation resettling technique
3. Time checks every 10 minutes when escalated
4. Continue until 6:45-7am
5. When 7am arrives: lights, food, socialization
6. Push through to scheduled nap time (don't bring nap earlier)

**Key Insight:** First nap timing can reinforce early rise - don't bring nap earlier than scheduled

## Voice and Tone Guidelines

**Apply Throughout:**
- Warm, confident, supportive
- Evidence-based but accessible
- Judgment-free
- Specific and actionable
- Realistic expectations
- Validation and encouragement

**Language:**
- "I would" / "I like to" / "For me"
- "You can" / "You don't have to"
- "Usually" / "Often" / "For most toddlers"
- "This is not because you've done anything wrong"
- "I know how hard this is"

**Avoid:**
- "You should" / "You must"
- "Always" / "Never"
- "Perfect" / "Flawless"
- Method war language
- Shaming language
