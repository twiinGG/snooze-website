### Observations

**Content & Flow**
*   The first viewport provides a clear, high-level summary of the problem and a "what to do first" hook.
*   The transition from educational content to the "Snooze Challenge" membership feels abrupt because the educational text ends without a specific bridge or "next step" call to action.
*   The "First steps" section is missing from the provided screenshots, making it difficult to judge the middle of the funnel.
*   The membership CTA is visually distinct but lacks a clear value proposition beyond the "Snooze Challenge" branding.

**Visual Design & Hierarchy**
*   The pages are highly repetitive. The layout, typography, and "Reviewed by" components are identical across all six topics.
*   The "Reviewed by" component is visually heavy and takes up significant vertical space, potentially pushing the primary content off-screen.
*   The "Snooze Challenge" logo/button is placed in a floating container that feels disconnected from the page flow.
*   The imagery at the bottom of the final pages is cut off, creating an unfinished look.

**Consistency & Differentiation**
*   The shared family resemblance is strong, but the pages are currently interchangeable. There is no visual cue to distinguish "Bedtime Battles" from "Night Wakings" other than the headline text.
*   The white space is generous, but the lack of varied visual elements makes the journey feel like a series of static cards rather than a cohesive narrative.

### Recommendations

**Shared Section Sequence**
1.  **Hero:** Problem Statement + "What to do first" summary.
2.  **Educational Deep Dive:** 3-4 bullet points of actionable advice.
3.  **The Bridge:** A "Why this is happening" section that connects the problem to the need for a structured plan.
4.  **The Solution:** Snooze Challenge membership CTA with a "Join the Challenge" button.

**High-Value Visual Changes**
1.  **Contextual Imagery:** Replace the generic "Snooze Challenge" logo in the CTA with a small, topic-specific illustration (e.g., a moon for night wakings, a sun for early rising) to differentiate the pages.
2.  **Dynamic Progress Indicators:** Add a small "Step 1 of 6" or a progress bar at the top to show the user where they are in the Snooze Challenge journey.
3.  **Refined Review Component:** Reduce the padding and size of the "Reviewed by" box to reclaim vertical space for the primary educational content.
