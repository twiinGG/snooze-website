## Navigation ruling

Use `Start here` as the universal decision path. Keep `Get help` for problem and age intent. Keep `Ways to work with us` for offer comparison. Put `Log in` outside the acquisition hierarchy as a utility action.

Do not put every product in the primary navigation. The navigation should help visitors choose a route, then let hub pages handle the detailed offer ladder.

## Internal-link pattern

Each age or problem page should offer three next steps:

1. A relevant free resource or article for low intent.
2. Snooze Membership as the default ongoing support route.
3. Camp Snooze or a consultation when the visitor wants faster or more hands-on help.

This pattern supports the current offer strategy: membership first, Camp as the higher-support secondary offer and consultations for high-intent cases.
