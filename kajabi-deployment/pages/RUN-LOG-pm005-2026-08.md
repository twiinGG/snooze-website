primary page and recorded it as a stale access model contradicting the ratified pricing. Wrong on both

`STATE CORRECTION: "Includes one month of Snooze access" recorded as a stale claim that predates the
ratified structure -> it is the correct and intended sell to a non-member. The camp price is the price,
Snooze access is included, and the setup-plus-subscription split is behind-the-scenes plumbing that exists

Worth naming why I got it wrong, because the same reasoning would repeat. I read the live offer as
"A$878 setup plus A$119 per month" and treated that structure as the offer. It is the billing shape.
The offer is what the buyer is being sold, and those are not the same thing. A verified fact about the
payment processor is not automatically a fact about the proposition.
