Thank you so much for choosing this guide. It's a privilege for me to support you and your baby in discovering better sleep. I have spent my entire life, professionally and privately, dedicated to the art of better sleep.

What This Guide Is About

This guide aims to give you confidence and nurture you as you navigate the fourth trimester, those first twelve weeks when everything feels new and you're learning your baby as they adjust to life outside the womb. This guide is not about sleep training or rigid scheduling. I wrote this guide in the hopes that it will feel like I'm there with you at 3am on the couch as you feed your new baby.

Important note: Your new baby cannot be spoiled. I want you to block out that noise as you read through this guide and trust me.

What You'll Learn

This comprehensive guide covers everything you need to understand and support your newborn's sleep:

  • Understanding newborn sleep: How sleep cycles work, the difference between REM and non-REM sleep, and how melatonin and cortisol affect your baby's sleep
  • Reading your baby's cues: How to recognize tired signs before they become overtired, plus a complete wake windows table from birth through 18 months
  • Sleep totals by age: Day sleep, overnight sleep, and nap counts for each stage of the fourth trimester
  • Building rhythm and routine: How to create patterns and structure (including the 7am reset) without being militant, plus a simple bedtime routine
  • The sleep environment: Complete safe sleep guidelines (Red Nose), plus how to set up your nursery with white noise, temperature control, swaddling, and blackout
  • Sleep associations: Understanding unassisted vs assisted associations, how to use a sleepy phrase, and guidance on dummies/pacifiers
  • Gentle settling techniques: Le Pause, The Shush/Pat, Pick Up/Put Down, and Dr Harvey Karp's 5 S's, all suited to newborns
  • Meet your village: Expert guidance from a midwife & NICU nurse on feeding, a women's health physiotherapist on recovery, and a dietitian & nutritionist on fourth trimester nutrition
