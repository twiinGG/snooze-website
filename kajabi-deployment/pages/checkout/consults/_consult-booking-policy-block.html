Before you book, please note
  • Your fee covers preparation as well as the call. We reserve your appointment time, read your questionnaire and build your sleep plan before we meet.
  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided you have not yet submitted your questionnaire and your session is more than 48 hours away. After that, consults are non-refundable.
  • You can reschedule once at no charge with at least 24 hours notice. Not attending without rescheduling counts as delivered, and your plan is still sent to you.
  • Book within 60 days of purchase. Consults expire 6 months from purchase.
  • We may ask you to sign a short client agreement before your session. It covers the points above plus what a consultation can and cannot do.
