Follow-Up Consultation

Additional support after your initial consultation

Haven't had an initial consult with us yet? Book the Signature Consultation here.

What you'll get:

  • Review of how things have gone since your first consultation
  • Adjustments to your existing sleep plan
  • Support for new challenges, changes or regressions
  • Private virtual session with Sally or Bec

This is for clients who completed an initial consultation with us within the last three months.

Before you book, please note
  • Your fee covers preparation as well as the call. We reserve your appointment time, review your file and prepare your updated plan before we meet.
  • Full refund within 7 days of purchase, provided you have not yet submitted your questionnaire and your session is more than 48 hours away. After that, consults are non-refundable.
  • You can reschedule once at no charge with at least 24 hours notice. Not attending without rescheduling counts as delivered, and your plan is still sent to you.
  • Book within 60 days of purchase. Consults expire 6 months from purchase.
  • We may ask you to sign a short client agreement before your session. It covers the points above plus what a consultation can and cannot do.
All consultation times are held during AEST (Australian Eastern Standard Time).
