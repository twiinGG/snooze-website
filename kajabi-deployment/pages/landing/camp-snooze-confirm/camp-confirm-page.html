What to do this week

Three things, and the first one is the one people put off.

  1. 1

    Log in and get the app

    Sign in with the same email and password you used at checkout. Most families end up using the Kajabi app on their phone, because camp happens at 2am as often as it happens at 2pm.

    If your password does not work, use Forgot password on the login screen.

  2. 2

    Work through your Camp Packing List

    It is open to you now. It holds your agreement, your intake form and the short set of things to have ready before day one. Doing it early means camp gets spent on your baby rather than on paperwork.

    Open your Camp Packing List
  3. 3

    Wait for your Friday

    Your camp group and the full Snooze library open on the Friday before camp starts. That gives you the weekend to look around and post your first question before the first day proper.
