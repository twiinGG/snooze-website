# Annual Moment Event v1: Outline

**Owner:** PRD-LADDER (BM-001) section 5(c). Draft, awaiting Sally sign-off.
Host: Sally. Format: live or asynchronous webinar-style session. Length: 45 minutes.

## Purpose

Give settled members a reason and a moment to commit to the year. The event earns
the annual ask by being genuinely useful first; the offer is the last five minutes,
not the frame.

## Positioning

Sally is introduced as an internationally certified sleep consultant and former
paediatric nurse. No claims beyond the CU-001-approved language. No promise of live
Q&A as a standing service; any questions taken on the day are answered on the day,
framed as part of this event, not an ongoing entitlement.

## Structure (45 minutes)

1. **Welcome and the season we're in (5 min).**
   Sally opens on the real arc of sleep change: it's a season, not a week. Sets the
   frame that the families who get the most out of Snooze are the ones who give it a
   proper run.

2. **The teaching block (25 min).**
   One genuinely useful, standalone topic tied to the community moment (for example,
   holding sleep gains through a regression, travel, or a return to work). Practical,
   evidence-based, usable that night. This is the reason to attend even for a member
   who never upgrades.

3. **What a full year with Snooze looks like (7 min).**
   Walk through how members use the courses, guides, and community across a year, and
   where the team helps (Sally, her Snooze Specialists, the community forum, 1:1
   consultations and Camp Snooze for personalised support). No cadence promise, no
   round-the-clock human-support claim, no standing live-question-session promise
   (CU-001 P2, P3).

4. **The annual moment (5 min).**
   The honest offer. The annual plan, framed as "lock in the year", available at the
   registry annual price for the event window. Saving stated as a proportion (around
   a third less than paying monthly). See `offer-mechanics.md`.

5. **Close and next step (3 min).**
   One clear CTA to the annual checkout in the member's currency. Replay availability
   stated with its real expiry. No countdown theatrics.

## Talking-point guardrails (for Sally)

- Sleep change is structured with built-in flexibility for real life (illness,
  travel, older siblings). Do not soften it into the opposite of structured (CU-001
  P7).
- Cry-based techniques are part of the program; do not promise no crying.
- Support is Sally and her Snooze Specialists, plus the community forum and 1:1
  consultations. Response aim is within 24 hours, Monday to Friday. Never claim
  round-the-clock human availability (CU-001 P2).
- Never assume a member's result. Speak to what the membership gives access to and
  how the team helps, not a guaranteed outcome.
