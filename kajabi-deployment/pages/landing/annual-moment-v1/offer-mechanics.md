The only urgency is the real event window. The invitation and the annual moment are
tied to the live session and a stated replay window (recommended 72 hours after the
event). There is no manufactured scarcity: no fake countdown, no "spots running
out", no price that is claimed to disappear beyond the honest event window. If Sally
wants the annual framing to stay available after the window, that is fine; the copy
simply stops using the event as the reason.
