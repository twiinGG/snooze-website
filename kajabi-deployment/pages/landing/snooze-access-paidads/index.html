If the four-month change knocked you sideways

I’m here to rebrand the scary four-month regression as the perfect time to start getting better sleep. The change is permanent; your baby now has a more mature sleep cycle, like ours. So they wake at the end of each cycle and look to be resettled the way they were first put to sleep.

Once babies develop these new cycles, they can learn to link them on their own. That’s the work. It needs a clear plan, not a wait-it-out. And you don’t have to choose between suffering through it and crying it out; those are not your only two options.
