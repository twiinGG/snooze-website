Who wrote it

Sally is an internationally certified sleep consultant and former paediatric nurse. She has been travelling the world with babies since her eldest was six months old and they flew from Melbourne to New York for three weeks.

"This guide is not about trying to achieve perfect sleep while you're away. The goal is to have great sleep in the bank before you go, know which anchors are worth holding onto while you're travelling, and have the confidence to know that your baby's sleep skills travel with them."
