Questions?

What ages is this for?

Camp Snooze is designed for babies aged 6 months. If your baby is younger and you need support, see our age-specific courses or join snooze for resources and sleep coaches in your corner. If your baby is almost 6 months old, Email us and we will help make sure it is right for you.

What if my baby cries?

We'll guide you through a plan that matches your baby and your comfort level, then coach you through the tricky moments.

Do I have to attend live calls?

Live is best for real-time support. Replays are available, and you'll still need to implement the same day.

What if I've tried sleep training before?

Many parents have. Camp Snooze gives you daily coaching and troubleshooting so you don't get stuck when things wobble.

Is this only for nights?

We work on nights and naps. We'll prioritise what moves the needle fastest for your family.

What's included and what does it cost?
