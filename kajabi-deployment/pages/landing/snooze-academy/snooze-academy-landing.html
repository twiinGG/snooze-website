The Curriculum

12 Weeks to Certification

A structured, comprehensive program that takes you from foundational sleep science through to running your own consulting practice.

  • Sleep Science Fundamentals: developmental biology, circadian rhythms, sleep cycles by age
  • The Snooze Method Philosophy: evidence-based + empathetic approach, building parent confidence
  • Safe Sleep Practices: SIDS prevention, safe environments, evidence-based safety guidelines
  • Newborn Sleep (0-3 months): fourth trimester, feeding and sleep relationship, foundations
  • The 3-4 Month Transition: regression management, schedule introduction, settling techniques
  • Infant Sleep (5-12 months): wake windows, responsive settling, 14-night implementation plans
  • Toddler Sleep (12+ months): nap transitions, bedtime battles, boundary setting
  • Client Assessment and Planning: intake processes, sleep logs, custom plan creation
  • Communication and Coaching Skills: tone of voice, managing emotional clients, boundaries
  • Building Your Practice: business setup, pricing strategy, marketing, social media
  • The Snooze Network: working within the ecosystem, lead referrals, community building
  • Certification Assessment: practical case studies, written assessment, supervised consultations
