Before you pack

A few things you might be wondering.

What age is Camp Snooze for?

Camp is designed for babies aged 5 months plus. If your baby is younger or you’re unsure about suitability, email us before booking.

Is Camp online?

Yes. Camp is a two-week virtual group experience with a personalised plan, coaching calls and a private forum.

Do I need to attend the calls live?

Live is best for real-time support. Replays are available, and you’ll still need to put your plan into practice that day. Roll call runs 10–11am Melbourne time.

What if I’ve tried sleep training before?

Many parents have. Daily coaching and troubleshooting give you somewhere to bring your questions when things don’t go as expected.

What happens after the two weeks?

Your included month of Snooze Membership starts the Friday before Camp and covers your two weeks at Camp. After that month, membership continues at your discounted rate. Cancel ongoing membership anytime.

Will my baby sleep through in two weeks?

There is no promise that every baby will sleep through by a particular date. Camp provides a personalised plan and two weeks of coaching to help you work on your baby’s sleep.
