Common Questions

Is this cry-it-out?

No. You can adapt everything to your comfort level. No pressure to follow methods that don't feel right.

What ages does Snooze work for?

Birth to 3 years. We have courses for newborns (0-3 months), 3-4 months, 5-12 months, and toddlers (12+ months).

How quickly will I see results?

Most families see improvements within the first week. Every baby is different, and the coaching helps you adjust as needed.

What if I don't have time?

Courses are self-paced. Coaching is optional. No pressure to keep up.

What if sleep gets worse before it gets better?

This is normal. You're not doing anything wrong. We help you adjust your plan as needed.
