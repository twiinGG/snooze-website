What to Expect

I know how overwhelming baby sleep can feel. Here's exactly what happens when you book a consultation, so you know what to expect every step of the way.

Before Your Consultation

You'll receive a pre-consultation questionnaire to complete. This helps me understand your baby's current sleep patterns, your goals, and any specific challenges you're facing. Take your time filling it out—the more detail you provide, the more personalised your plan will be.

During Your Consultation

We'll spend our time together discussing your baby's sleep, your parenting style, and what you want to achieve. I'll ask questions to understand the full picture, then we'll work together to create a plan that feels right for your family. This is a conversation, not a lecture—your input matters.

After Your Consultation

You'll receive your personalised sleep plan within 48 hours. This includes specific schedules, settling techniques, and step-by-step guidance tailored to your baby's age and your family's needs. For Signature consults, you'll also have 7 days of email support to ask questions as you implement the plan.

Implementation Support

Change takes time, and I'm here to support you through it. With the 2-Week Package, you'll have daily check-ins and immediate responses to challenges. For Signature consults, use your 7-day email support window to ask questions as you implement your plan.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age range do your sleep consultations cover?

My sleep consultations are designed for babies from newborn (0 months) up to 4 years and older. My 2-week consultation package is suitable for babies 4 months and older.

What is included in the 60-minute personal sleep consultation?

The Signature Consultation includes a comprehensive sleep assessment, detailed personalised sleep plan, guidance on creating an optimal sleep environment, and expert advice on addressing bedtime resistance, night wakings, and more. You'll also receive 7 days of email support after your consultation.

Can I book the 45-minute follow-up consultation without an initial consultation?

No, the 45-minute follow-up consultation is exclusively for returning clients who have already booked and completed an initial 60-minute consultation within the last three months.

Do you offer in-person consultations?

All consultations are held virtually, which ensures convenience and flexibility for parents worldwide. Virtual consultations allow me to work with families regardless of location, and many parents find them just as effective as in-person sessions.

What if I need help beyond sleep-related issues?

I have a network of specialists, including paediatricians, lactation consultants, nutritionists, and women's health physiotherapists, who I can refer you to if needed. That's why they call me The Sleep Concierge—I'm here to help point you in the right direction for all your family's needs.

How do I book a consultation?

Simply click the "Book Your Consultation" button on your preferred package above. After booking, you'll receive an email with further instructions to schedule your session, including a link to complete your pre-consultation questionnaire.

What if my baby doesn't respond to the sleep plan?

Every baby is unique, and progress may vary. During consultations, I provide personalised strategies and ongoing support to adapt the plan as needed. Patience and consistency are key to receiving better results. If you're on the 2-Week Package, we'll adjust daily. For Signature consults, use your 7-day email support to troubleshoot as you implement.

Do Snooze Members get a discount on consultations?

Yes. Snooze Members receive preferred pricing on all consultations. The Signature Consultation is $525 for members (compared to $650 standard), and the 2-Week Package is $3,150 for members (compared to $3,500 standard). Join Snooze to unlock member pricing.
