## PART 1: Kajabi Product Titles + Descriptions

### 🔹 Core Principle for All Products

* Avoid “membership”, “course”, “program” in titles where possible

* Use “access”, “support”, “coaching”, “guidance”

## ❓ Section 6: FAQs (Long-Tail SEO)

Keep these conversational and simple.

**What’s the difference between a baby sleep guide and ongoing support?**
Guides provide education. Ongoing support includes live guidance, feedback, and help applying that information to your baby.

**Do I need coaching or ongoing support?**
Many parents start with education and move into support when they want help implementing changes or navigating regressions.

**Can I change levels later?**
Yes. Families often move between guides, ongoing support, and coaching as their needs change.
