Below is a **comprehensive, build-ready brief** for the Snooze Store page that does two things at once:

1. Makes the **support hierarchy instantly legible through visuals, layout, and interaction**, not copy
2. Builds in a **mockup + symbolism exploration phase** before any final page is locked

This is written so you can hand it directly to a designer, dev, or keep it as your internal north star.

---

## SNOOZE STORE PAGE

### Visual Hierarchy & Support Architecture Brief

### Primary Objective

Design a Store page that visually communicates Snooze’s **graduated support system**, positioning Snooze as a *support infrastructure*, not a set of products.

A visitor should immediately understand:

* What level of support they’re looking at
* How each option relates to the others
* Why Snooze is the centre of gravity

No comparison tables as the main device. No “telling.” The page should *show*.

---

## Core Concept: “Levels of Support, One System”

The Store is not a catalogue. It’s a **map**.

We are visually expressing:

* Depth over breadth
* Escalation, not choice overload
* Safety, containment, and progression

Think: *scaffolding*, *layers*, *concentric systems*, *gravity*, *base → reinforcement → intensive support*.

---

## PAGE INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE (HIGH LEVEL)

1. Hero: “Choose Your Level of Support”
2. Visual Hierarchy Section (the heart of the page)
3. Deep Dive on Each Layer (stacked, not side-by-side)
4. Reassurance & Pathways (“You don’t have to decide now”)
5. SEO & AI reinforcement blocks
6. Soft CTA (not salesy)

---

## SECTION 1: HERO — ORIENTATION, NOT CONVERSION

### Goal

Orient the user to *how* to think about Snooze before asking them to choose anything.

### Visual Direction

* Clean, calm, spacious
* No pricing yet
* No buttons competing for attention

### Headline (example direction)

**“Support for Baby Sleep, at Every Level”**

Sub-line:
**“From self-guided foundations to hands-on support, Snooze is designed to meet you where you are.”**

Optional micro-line:
Evidence-based. Age-specific. Human-led.

---

## SECTION 2: THE VISUAL HIERARCHY (MOST IMPORTANT SECTION)

This is where we replace explanation with *design*.

### Core Requirement

The hierarchy must be understandable **without reading a single paragraph**.

### Mockup Phase Required (Non-Negotiable)

Before final design, explore **3 visual metaphors**, mocked at low fidelity.

#### Concept A: Concentric Circles (Recommended Primary Direction)

* Snooze at the centre
* Outer rings represent deeper / more intensive support
* Visual metaphor: safety, containment, village, nucleus

Example structure:

* Centre: Snooze Membership (Core Support)
* Ring 2: Camp Snooze (Guided, time-bound)
* Ring 3: 1:1 Consult (Highest intensity)

Why this works:

* Instantly communicates “everything flows through Snooze”
* Avoids “tier ladder” price psychology
* Signals escalation, not upsell

#### Concept B: Vertical Support Stack

* Bottom = always-on support
* Middle = structured intensives
* Top = personalised escalation

Visual metaphor: foundation → reinforcement → intervention

This can work well on mobile if circles get tricky.

#### Concept C: Pathway / Journey Map

* A single vertical path with widening support
* Less about time, more about depth

This is softer but risks implying linear progression. Use only if A or B fails.

**Deliverable for mockup phase:**
3 wireframes (no colour, no imagery polish), tested internally for instant clarity.

---

## SECTION 3: THE LAYERS (STACKED, NOT COMPARED)

Each layer is revealed **top to bottom**, not side by side.

### Layer 1: Snooze Membership (Anchor Layer)

This must visually feel:

* Largest
* Calmest
* Most stable

Design cues:

* Wider container
* Softer background
* More white space
* Iconography that signals “home base”

Content focus:

* “This is where most families start”
* Emphasis on *ongoing*, *contained*, *confidence-building* support
* No urgency language

CTA:
“Explore Snooze”

---

### Layer 2: Camp Snooze (Intensive, Time-Bound)

Visually:

* Slightly narrower
* More structured
* Clear time boundary (e.g. “2-week intensive”)

Design cues:

* Dashed outlines
* Calendar motifs
* Progress indicators

Messaging focus:

* “For families who want hands-on guidance and momentum”
* Clearly positioned as **inside** the Snooze ecosystem, not separate

CTA:
“See Camp Snooze”

---

### Layer 3: 1:1 Consults (Highest Touch)

Visually:

* Smallest surface area
* Most deliberate
* Premium restraint

Design cues:

* Strong framing
* Minimal copy
* Emphasis on intentionality

Messaging focus:

* Complexity
* Personalisation
* Escalation when needed

CTA:
“Book a Consult”

---

## SECTION 4: REASSURANCE — “YOU DON’T HAVE TO CHOOSE PERFECTLY”

This section prevents decision paralysis.

Visual:

* Simple checklist or flow
* Calm tone

Content direction:

* “Most families start with Snooze”
* “You can move between levels”
* “We’ll guide you if you’re unsure”

This mirrors how Good Inside reduces pressure, but grounded in Snooze’s service model.

---

## SECTION 5: SEO + AI OPTIMISATION (BUILT IN, NOT BOLTED ON)

### On-Page Structure

* One clear H1
* Semantic H2s per support level
* Natural language explanations that answer:

  * What is baby sleep support?
  * How does sleep coaching work?
  * What’s the difference between courses and coaching?

### AI Discoverability

* Plain-language summaries under each section
* FAQ block written as full sentences, not fragments
* Avoid jargon. Write as if explaining to a smart friend.

---

## SECTION 6: FINAL CTA (LOW PRESSURE)

Not “Buy now”.

Directional language:

* “Get support”
* “See what’s right for your family”
* “Start with Snooze”

---

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES (NON-NEGOTIABLE)

* Hierarchy beats persuasion
* White space is authority
* Fewer buttons, clearer pathways
* No price anchoring visuals until after hierarchy is understood
* Mobile clarity is more important than desktop polish

---

## SUCCESS CRITERIA

You’ll know this worked if:

* People stop asking “what’s the difference between…”
* Store page sessions increase but bounce rate drops
* Users flow *from* Store → Snooze → deeper offers naturally
* The page reads like infrastructure, not marketing
