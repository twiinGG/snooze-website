Common Questions

What's included in my Snooze membership?

Your membership gives you access to every Snooze course and guide (0–3 years), live masterclasses and coaching sessions, a full library of catch up recordings and the Snooze Village community.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel any time from your account settings. Your membership will stay active for the remainder of your billing period.

What if Snooze doesn't work for us?

If Snooze isn't the right fit, you can request a refund within 7 days.

How is Snooze different from other sleep programs?

Snooze combines expert-led coaching, on-demand learning, and community support. Created by an internationally certified sleep consultant and former paediatric nurse. You're not doing this alone.

Do I need to follow a strict routine?

The routine matters. Most of the work happens during the daytime. Our approach focusses on getting the day right and then allowing bedtime to follow. That does not mean rigid. Life with a baby brings the unpredictable: childcare, sickness and older siblings. So we troubleshoot the plan with you when things do not go to plan. Structure first, flexibility through support.

I've tried other sleep help before. Will this be different?

Many parents come to Snooze after trying apps, courses, or other consultants. The difference is you're not alone with the information. You get structured support through the Snooze Village, live masterclasses and coaching sessions, and guidance when you need help applying the methods in Snooze to your specific situation.
