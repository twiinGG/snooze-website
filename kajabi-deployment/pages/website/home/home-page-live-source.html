Quick Answers Before You Join

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel any time from your account settings. Your membership will stay active for the remainder of your billing period.

We offer a range of evidence-based approaches. You choose what aligns with your parenting style. There's no one-size-fits-all method.

What ages does Snooze cover?

Babies and toddlers from 0 to 3 years. We have specific courses for each stage: Newborn, 3-4 Month, 5-12 Month, and Toddler.

How fast do you respond?

The Snooze Village is monitored during business hours (AEST). Wayfinding responses within 2 hours, program channel support within 4 hours.

Common Questions

What's included in my Snooze membership?

Your membership gives you access to every Snooze course and guide (0–3 years), weekly group coaching, replays, and our supportive community.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. You can cancel any time from your account settings. Your membership will stay active for the remainder of your billing period.

What if Snooze doesn't work for us?

If Snooze isn't the right fit, you can request a refund within 7 days.

How is Snooze different from other sleep programs?

Snooze combines expert-led coaching, on-demand learning, and community support. Created by a paediatric nurse and certified sleep consultant. You're not doing this alone.

Do I need to follow a strict routine?

Not at all. Snooze helps you understand sleep patterns so you can adapt routines to your baby's needs and your lifestyle.

I've tried other sleep help before. Will this be different?

Many parents come to Snooze after trying apps, courses, or other consultants. The difference is you're not alone with the information. You get structured support through the Snooze Village, weekly live coaching, and guidance when you need help applying the method to your specific situation.
