# Design notes for Kajabi (so it looks premium fast)

* Use generous spacing and keep paragraphs short (2–3 lines).
* Keep buttons consistent with your red/pink brand button.
* Use cards for Topics and Latest Answers, with plenty of white space.
* Avoid big blocks of bio or philosophy here, that lives on /about-sally.
