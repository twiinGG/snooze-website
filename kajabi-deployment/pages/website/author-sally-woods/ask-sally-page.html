Do you answer every question?

I read every submission. I can't publish every one, but I use them to choose what I write next.

No. It's education and practical support. If you're worried about your child's health, check in with your doctor.

Can I submit a question?

Yes. Use the question box below. If it's a common issue, I'll write an Ask Sally answer.

Where do I start if I'm overwhelmed?

Start with your biggest pain point, usually night wakes or naps. Then follow the steps in the post for 7 days.
