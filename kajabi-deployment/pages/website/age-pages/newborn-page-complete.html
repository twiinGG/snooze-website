Understanding Newborn Sleep

What you're experiencing is normal. Newborn sleep patterns are still developing—this isn't something you need to 'fix' immediately.

What's Normal

If your newborn is waking every 2 hours, having short naps, or seems confused about day and night—this is completely normal.

Usually Normal

  • Waking every 2-3 hours for feeds
  • Short 30-45 minute naps
  • Day/night confusion
  • Needing help to fall asleep

Might Need Attention

  • Questions about safe sleep setup
  • Persistent sleep deprivation affecting your wellbeing
  • Extreme fussiness that doesn't respond to comfort
  • Medical concerns (reflux, feeding issues)

Remember: Most newborn sleep patterns are biological and will improve as your baby grows. You're not doing anything wrong.
