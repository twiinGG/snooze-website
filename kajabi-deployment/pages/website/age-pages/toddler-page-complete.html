Understanding Toddler Sleep

Toddler sleep challenges are often developmental. Understanding what's normal helps you know when to address vs. when to wait it out.

What's Normal

Most toddlers go through phases of challenging sleep, boundary testing, and separation anxiety; this is all part of normal development.

Usually Normal

  • Bedtime battles and resistance
  • Nap transitions (2 to 1 nap)
  • 18-month sleep regression
  • Separation anxiety (18-24 months)

Might Need Attention

  • Consistent boundary setting needed
  • Routine and schedule protection
  • Managing transitions and regressions
  • Sleep schedule flexibility guidance

Remember: Bedtime battles are how toddlers learn boundaries. Consistent routines and clear expectations can help manage this; it's usually addressable.
