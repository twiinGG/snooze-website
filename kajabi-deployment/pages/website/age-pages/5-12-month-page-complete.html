Understanding 5-12 Month Sleep

By now, your baby can learn independent sleep skills. Understanding what's developmental vs. what's a habit helps you know when to address sleep challenges.

What's Normal

By 5-6 months, most babies are developmentally ready to learn independent sleep skills. Challenges are normal, but this is when you can make lasting changes.

Usually Normal

  • Early rising (before 6am)
  • Nap transitions (3 to 2 naps)
  • Short naps (30-45 minutes)
  • Separation anxiety (8-10 months)

Might Need Attention

  • Schedule and wake window adjustments
  • Sleep training readiness
  • Night weaning guidance
  • Persistent early rising despite schedule changes

Remember: Most early rising and nap challenges can be addressed with schedule adjustments. This is usually fixable, not a permanent trait.
