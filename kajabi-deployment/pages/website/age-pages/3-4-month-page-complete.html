Understanding 3-4 Month Sleep

The changes you're seeing are a sign of growth, not a problem. Your baby's sleep is maturing; this is actually good news.

What's Normal

If your baby's sleep has suddenly changed, you're experiencing the "4-month sleep regression." This is actually a permanent maturation of sleep cycles; not something that will pass.

Usually Normal

  • Waking more frequently overnight
  • More wakeful periods
  • Nap consolidation (4 to 3 naps)
  • Needing help to fall asleep

Might Need Attention

  • Ready to establish healthy sleep habits
  • Persistent sleep deprivation affecting wellbeing
  • Questions about sleep training readiness
  • Schedule and wake window guidance needed

Key Insight: The four-month sleep regression is actually the perfect opportunity to start establishing healthy sleep habits. Your baby's sleep is permanently changing; this is the ideal time to help them learn independent sleep skills.
