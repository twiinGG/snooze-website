Course Overview

This foundational course gives you a complete framework for understanding and supporting your child's sleep at any age. Rather than offering quick fixes, you'll build a solid understanding of how sleep works and develop the confidence to adapt strategies to your unique family situation. Whether you're just starting out or feel like you've tried everything, this course helps you think differently about sleep challenges.

What You'll Learn

  • How to identify root causes rather than treating symptoms
  • A flexible framework adaptable to changing sleep needs
  • Why context matters more than rigid rules
  • How to evaluate sleep advice against your family values
  • Practical troubleshooting without second-guessing
  • Distinguishing intervention needs vs. natural resolution
  • Building sustainable habits for the whole family
  • Confidence to make informed sleep decisions

Course Format

  • Text-based lessons for easy reading
  • Downloadable PDF guides for offline reference
  • Reflection prompts for specific situations
  • Practical frameworks and decision tools
  • 4-6 resources including checklists
  • Self-paced learning for unpredictable schedules

Time Investment

  • Self-paced learning, no completion deadline
  • Approximately 2-3 hours total content
  • Flexible completion, dip in and out
  • Reference back anytime challenges arise
  • Lifetime access to revisit as child grows

Course Curriculum

Complete module-by-module breakdown. Click each module to see detailed lessons, learning objectives, and included resources.

Module 1: Introduction to The Snooze Method

4 lessons • Foundation
+

Wondering what actually makes a sleep approach work? This module explains what The Snooze Method is, the evidence-based foundation behind it, and why it's different from other sleep training approaches you may have tried. You'll understand the 'why' behind everything we teach, which helps you feel confident applying it to your own family.

Module 2: Core Philosophy and Mindset

4 lessons • Mindset
+

If you've ever felt overwhelmed by conflicting sleep advice, this module is for you. You'll explore the beliefs and flexible approach that guide all Snooze guidance, and discover why parent confidence matters just as much as any technique. This helps you filter advice, trust your instincts, and balance structure with the flexibility real life demands.

Module 3: Thinking About Sleep Challenges

4 lessons • Analysis
+

Not sure whether your baby's sleep is normal or something that needs attention? This module gives you a framework for understanding when to act versus when to wait. You'll learn to distinguish typical developmental patterns from behavioral sleep challenges, and recognise what's actually a sleep association versus what's just normal baby sleep.

Module 4: Adapting The Method to Your Family

4 lessons • Application
+

Every family is different, and rigid advice rarely works in real life. This module shows you how to apply The Snooze Method principles to your unique situation, whether that's adapting sleep training to your family's schedule, balancing flexibility with structure, or knowing when it's time to seek additional support.

How to Use This Course

Practical guidance on getting the most out of this course. Learn at your own pace and reference back as needed.

Self-Paced Learning

Complete the course at your own speed. Some parents finish in one session, others prefer to work through modules over several days or weeks. There's no deadline or rush.

Recommended Order

Modules are designed to be completed sequentially, as each builds on the previous. However, you can jump to specific topics if you have urgent questions. All content remains available for reference.

Implementation Timeline

You can start implementing strategies immediately, even while still watching lessons. Many parents begin with environment setup and routines, then move to settling techniques when ready.

Reference Back Anytime

All course content remains accessible for lifetime. Reference specific lessons, download resources again, or review modules as your baby grows and changes.
