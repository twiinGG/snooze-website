Course Overview

This course provides comprehensive guidance for navigating the unique sleep challenges that come with toddlerhood. You'll learn evidence-based strategies for handling bedtime battles, managing nap transitions, and working through the big changes, like new siblings or moving to a big kid bed, that can throw sleep off track. Whether you're dealing with a toddler who's suddenly fighting bedtime or one who thinks 5am is morning, this toolkit gives you practical approaches that work with your toddler's developing independence.

What You'll Learn

  • Understanding toddler sleep behaviors
  • Age-appropriate schedules & routines
  • Managing nap transitions (2-to-1)
  • Handling bedtime battles & resistance
  • Resolving early rising & night wakings
  • Navigating big transitions (crib-to-bed)

Course Format

Course Curriculum

Complete module-by-module breakdown. Click each module to see detailed lessons, learning objectives, and included resources.

Module 1: Understanding Toddler Sleep

Foundations
+

If you're wondering why toddler sleep feels so different from baby sleep, or whether your little one's bedtime battles are normal, this module explains what's really going on. You'll learn how toddler sleep works, what behaviors are developmentally expected versus what needs attention, and when it might be time to seek help. A helpful foundation for navigating common toddler sleep problems with confidence.

Module 2: Age-Appropriate Schedules and Routines

Schedules
+

Trying to figure out the right toddler sleep schedule for your 18-month-old, or wondering how to make daycare naps work? This module gives you flexible, age-appropriate schedules that adapt to real life, including daycare, travel, and illness. You'll get practical routines you can actually stick to, even when things don't go to plan.

Module 3: Nap Transitions

Transitions
+

If you're facing the 2-to-1 nap transition or wondering when your toddler will drop their nap entirely, this module walks you through each stage. You'll learn how to spot readiness signs, manage nap refusal, and troubleshoot common setbacks. Helpful for parents navigating these tricky transitions without losing their sanity, or their toddler's sleep.

Module 4: Bedtime Battles and Resistance

Behavior
+

Dealing with a toddler who won't go to bed or drags out bedtime for hours? This module gives you practical tools for handling bedtime battles, including scripts for common stalling tactics and calm boundary-setting techniques. You'll learn how to create a bedtime routine for toddlers that actually works, even when separation anxiety shows up.

Module 5: Early Rising and Night Wakings

Troubleshooting
+

If your toddler is waking at 5am or calling out multiple times each night, this module helps you understand why, and what to do about it. You'll get clear protocols for toddler early rising, strategies for night wakings, and guidance on nightmares versus night terrors. Practical help for exhausted parents ready for longer stretches of sleep.

Module 6: Big Transitions

Milestones
+

Wondering when to tackle the crib to bed transition, or how to handle toddler sleep regressions without undoing all your progress? This module covers major sleep transitions, including travel disruptions and routine changes. You'll learn how to navigate these shifts smoothly so sleep doesn't completely fall apart during big life moments.

Module 7: Troubleshooting and Advanced Strategies

Advanced
+

When you've tried everything and toddler sleep problems still aren't improving, this module offers advanced strategies and fresh approaches. You'll learn how to troubleshoot what's not working, maintain the progress you've made, and recognise when it might be time for additional support. Helpful for parents who need that extra level of guidance.

How to Use This Course

Practical guidance on getting the most out of this course. Learn at your own pace and reference back as needed.

Self-Paced Learning

Complete the course at your own speed. Some parents finish in one session, others prefer to work through modules over several days or weeks. There's no deadline or rush.

Recommended Order

Modules are designed to be completed sequentially, as each builds on the previous. However, you can jump to specific topics if you have urgent questions. All content remains available for reference.

Implementation Timeline

You can start implementing strategies immediately, even while still watching lessons. Many parents begin with environment setup and routines, then move to settling techniques when ready.

Reference Back Anytime

All course content remains accessible on demand. Reference specific lessons, download resources again, or review modules as your baby grows and changes.

Is This Course Right for You?

This course is designed for parents of babies 12+ months who are experiencing sleep challenges or want to prepare for upcoming changes.

  • Best for: Babies 12+ months experiencing sleep challenges, or parents wanting to build better sleep foundations.
  • Time commitment: Self-paced learning. Estimated 2-4 hours to complete all content, but can be spread over days or weeks.
  • What you'll need: Willingness to implement strategies, time to complete checklists, and patience as changes take time to establish.
  • Not the right fit? If your baby is outside the 12+ months window, check out our 5-12 Month Course or 3-4 Month Course instead.
