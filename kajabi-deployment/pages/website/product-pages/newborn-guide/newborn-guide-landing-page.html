Guide Overview

This guide is your companion through the fourth trimester, those first twelve weeks when everything feels new and you're learning your baby as they adjust to life outside the womb. You'll find practical, evidence-based strategies for understanding newborn sleep patterns, recognising when your baby needs rest, and creating foundations that support sleep without rigid rules. Whether you're wondering what's normal, feeling overwhelmed by conflicting advice, or simply want to feel more confident in those early weeks, this guide meets you where you are.

What You'll Learn

  • Understanding newborn sleep cycles & biology
  • Reading subtle tired signs vs overtired cues
  • Realistic wake windows for 0-3 months
  • Settling techniques (Shush/Pat, 5 S's)
  • Setting up a safe sleep environment
  • The "7am Reset" and flexible routines

Guide Contents

A complete module-by-module breakdown of the guide. Click each module to see what is covered in this comprehensive resource.

Part 1: Understanding Sleep

Module 1: Introduction & About The Guide

Philosophy • Confidence Building
+

Wondering if you're already doing something wrong with your newborn's sleep? This module shares my philosophy for the fourth trimester and why this guide isn't about sleep training, it's about understanding your baby. You'll learn why you genuinely cannot spoil a newborn and how to build confidence in your instincts. This is your starting point for newborn sleep help that actually feels right.

Module 2: What is Sleep and Why Do You Need It?

Sleep Science • Biology
+

Why does your newborn seem to sleep so differently from you? This module breaks down how newborn sleep cycles work, including REM and non-REM sleep, plus the hormones like melatonin and cortisol that drive your baby's patterns. Understanding the science behind newborn sleep patterns helps you stop fighting against biology and start working with it.

Module 3: Tired Signs

Cues • Overtiredness
+

Is your baby overtired, or not tired enough? This module teaches you how to read your newborn's sleep cues, the subtle "sleep ready" signs versus the harder-to-settle overtired signals. You'll learn my pro tips for recognising tired signs before that overtired window hits, making settling so much easier for both of you.

Module 4: Wake Windows

0-3 Months • Sleep Totals
+

How long can your newborn actually stay awake? This module covers wake window guidelines from 0-3 months, including realistic sleep totals for day and night. You'll get age-specific recommendations for nap frequency and duration, so you can stop guessing whether your newborn sleep schedule is on track for their stage.

Module 5: Rhythm and Routine

7am Reset • Bedtime Routine
+

Craving some predictability without rigid schedules? This module shows you how to create patterns that work with your newborn's natural rhythm. You'll learn about the 7am Reset concept and simple bedtime routine components that signal sleep time. A fourth trimester routine can bring calm without the pressure of strict timings.

Part 2: Practical Tools & Support

Module 6: The Sleep Environment

Safety • Room Setup
+

Is your nursery actually set up for safe, restful sleep? This module covers Red Nose safe sleep guidelines alongside practical room setup, white noise, temperature, swaddling, and blackout requirements. You'll feel confident that your newborn sleep environment supports both safety and better sleep without second-guessing every detail.

Module 7: Sleep Associations

Habits • Dummies • Sleepy Phrases
+

Worried you're creating "bad habits" with your newborn? This module explains the difference between assisted and unassisted sleep associations, and why both have their place in the fourth trimester. You'll get practical guidance on sleepy phrases and dummy use, so you can support your baby's sleep without unnecessary guilt.

Module 8: Settling & Re-Settling

Techniques • 5 S's
+

How do you actually get a newborn to sleep? This module walks through proven settling techniques including Le Pause, Shush/Pat, Pick Up/Put Down, and Dr Harvey Karp's 5 S's. You'll learn when cuddling your baby to sleep is completely okay, because in the fourth trimester, sometimes that's exactly what they need.

Module 9: Meet Your Village

Expert Interviews • Postpartum Care
+

Need support beyond sleep? This module brings in expert guidance from specialists who understand the fourth trimester. You'll hear from a NICU nurse on feeding and reflux, a pelvic floor physio on your postpartum recovery, and a dietitian on nourishing yourself. Because newborn care means looking after you too.

How to Use This Guide

Practical guidance on getting the most out of this guide. Read at your own pace and reference back as needed.

Self-Paced Reading

Read through the guide at your own speed. Some parents finish in one session, others prefer to work through modules over several days or weeks. There's no deadline or rush.

Recommended Order

Modules are designed to be completed sequentially, as each builds on the previous. However, you can jump to specific topics if you have urgent questions. All content remains available for reference.

Implementation Timeline

You can start implementing strategies immediately, even while still watching lessons. Many parents begin with environment setup and routines, then move to settling techniques when ready.

Reference Back Anytime

All guide content remains accessible on demand. Reference specific modules, download resources again, or review sections as your baby grows and changes.

Is This Guide Right for You?

This guide is designed for parents of babies 0-3 months who are experiencing sleep challenges or want to prepare for upcoming changes.

  • Best for: Babies 0-3 months experiencing sleep challenges, or parents wanting to build better sleep foundations.
  • Time commitment: Self-paced learning. Estimated 2-4 hours to complete all content, but can be spread over days or weeks.
  • What you'll need: Willingness to implement strategies, time to complete checklists, and patience as changes take time to establish.
  • Not the right fit? If your baby is outside the 0-3 months window, check out our 3-4 Month Course or 5-12 Month Course instead.
