Course Overview

This course walks you through everything you need to know about your baby's sleep from 5-12 months, a time when sleep often feels unpredictable but is actually full of opportunity. You'll find clear, evidence-based strategies for both night sleep and naps, along with practical tools to help you create a plan that fits your family. Whether you're dealing with frequent night wakings, short naps, or simply want to feel more confident about your approach, this course gives you the structure and flexibility to move forward.

Course Curriculum

Complete module-by-module breakdown. Click each module to see detailed lessons, learning objectives, and included resources.

Part 1: The Prep

Module 1: Understanding Your Baby's Sleep

Foundational knowledge • Sleep cues
+

Wondering when your baby is ready for sleep training, or struggling to tell tired signs from overtired? This module covers how baby sleep cycles actually work, how to read your baby's unique cues, and how to prepare yourself emotionally for the journey ahead. It's the foundation that makes everything else click into place.

Module 2: Optimising the Sleep Environment

Safe sleep • Sleep associations
+

Not sure if your baby's room is too hot, too cold, or set up in a way that's actually working against you? Here you'll learn exactly how to create a safe sleep setup, from ideal room temperature for baby to managing sleep associations that support (rather than sabotage) independent sleep. Small changes here often make a surprisingly big difference.

Module 3: Routines, Schedules & Feeding

Daily structure • Feeding guides
+

If you're googling "5 month old schedule" or battling early rising that's throwing off your whole day, this module is for you. You'll learn how to structure feeding schedules and daily routines that set up better nights, because what happens during the day shapes what happens after dark.

Part 2: The Plan

Module 4: The Night Sleep Plan

Sleep training methods • Night weaning
+

Ready to actually make changes but unsure which sleep training methods suit your family? This module walks you through the techniques step-by-step, including the soothe and support method and guidance on night weaning. You'll have a clear plan you can adapt to your baby and your comfort level.

Module 5: The Nap Training Plan

Nap extension • Nap transitions
+

Naps can feel like a completely different beast, short naps, fighting sleep, or wondering how to handle the 3 to 2 nap transition. This module shows you how to train baby to nap independently, extend those frustrating short naps, and navigate nap transitions without losing the progress you've made at night.

Module 6: Troubleshooting & Success

Regressions • Illness • Travel
+

What happens when sleep training setbacks pop up, teething, illness, travel, or sudden baby sleep problems you didn't expect? This module helps you troubleshoot the bumps, understand what's normal, and maintain your progress long-term. Because real life doesn't pause, and your plan shouldn't fall apart when it gets messy.

How to Use This Course

Practical guidance on getting the most out of this course. Learn at your own pace and reference back as needed.

Self-Paced Learning

Complete the course at your own speed. Some parents finish in one session, others prefer to work through modules over several days or weeks. There's no deadline or rush.

Recommended Order

Modules are designed to be completed sequentially, as each builds on the previous. However, you can jump to specific topics if you have urgent questions. All content remains available for reference.

Implementation Timeline

You can start implementing strategies immediately, even while still watching lessons. Many parents begin with environment setup and routines, then move to settling techniques when ready.

Reference Back Anytime

All course content remains accessible on demand. Reference specific lessons, download resources again, or review modules as your baby grows and changes.

Is This Course Right for You?

This course is designed for parents of babies 5-12 months who are experiencing sleep challenges or want to prepare for upcoming changes.

  • Best for: Babies 5-12 months experiencing sleep challenges, or parents wanting to build better sleep foundations.
  • Time commitment: Self-paced learning. Estimated 2-4 hours to complete all content, but can be spread over days or weeks.
  • What you'll need: Willingness to implement strategies, time to complete checklists, and patience as changes take time to establish.
  • Not the right fit? If your baby is outside the 5-12 months window, check out our Newborn Sleep Guide or Toddler Toolkit instead.
