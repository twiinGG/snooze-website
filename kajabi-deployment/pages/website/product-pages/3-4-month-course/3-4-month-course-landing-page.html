Course Curriculum

Complete module-by-module breakdown. Click each module to see detailed lessons, learning objectives, and included resources.

Part 1: The Prep

Module 1: Understanding 3-4 Month Sleep Changes

5 lessons • 45 minutes • 3 resources
+

If you're wondering "why is my 4-month-old sleeping worse?" or "is this the 4-month sleep regression?", this module explains what's really happening. You'll learn why sleep cycles permanently change at this age, why your baby might be waking every hour, and what's normal versus what needs attention. Perfect for parents experiencing the 4-month sleep regression or wondering when to start sleep training.

Module 2: Creating Optimal Sleep Environment

3 lessons • 25 minutes • 2 resources
+

Struggling with short naps or frequent night wakings? Your baby's sleep environment plays a bigger role than you might think. This module covers safe sleep setup, blackout curtains for better naps, and white noise to help your baby sleep longer. You'll get step-by-step guidance on room temperature, lighting, and creating a sleep space that supports longer, more restful sleep.

Module 3: Schedules & Routines

4 lessons • 35 minutes • 4 resources
+

Wondering about 3-4 month wake windows or how to handle short naps? This module gives you flexible, realistic schedules that work in real life. You'll learn age-appropriate wake windows, how to adjust when naps are short, and how to create routines that don't feel rigid. Perfect for parents asking "when should I start sleep training?" or dealing with unpredictable nap lengths.

Part 2: The Plan

Module 4: Settling Techniques

5 lessons • 50 minutes • 3 resources
+

If your baby only falls asleep with feeding, rocking, or holding, this module shows you how to teach self-settling at 4 months old. You'll learn settling techniques, when to start sleep training, and how to help your baby fall asleep independently. This addresses the core of the 4-month regression: babies who wake at the end of each sleep cycle and need help resettling the way they first fell asleep.

Module 5: Night Wakes & Feeding

4 lessons • 40 minutes • 2 resources
+

Dealing with a 4-month-old waking every hour or multiple times at night? This module helps you understand whether night wakings are due to hunger, habit, or the sleep regression. You'll learn how to distinguish feeding needs from resettling needs, when overnight feeds are still necessary, and strategies to reduce frequent night wakings. Perfect for parents asking "how can I stop night wakings?" or "why does my baby wake every 2 hours?"

Module 6: Extending Naps

3 lessons • 30 minutes • 2 resources
+

Frustrated with 30-minute cat naps? This module explains why short naps happen at 3-4 months and gives you practical techniques to help your baby sleep longer. You'll learn about sleep cycles, the Le Pause technique, and the Cot-Hour strategy to extend naps. Ideal for parents dealing with nap resistance, short daytime sleep, or wondering how to consolidate naps at this age.

How to Use This Course

Practical guidance on getting the most out of this course. Learn at your own pace and reference back as needed.

Self-Paced Learning

Complete the course at your own speed. Some parents finish in one session, others prefer to work through modules over several days or weeks. There's no deadline or rush.

Recommended Order

Modules are designed to be completed sequentially, as each builds on the previous. However, you can jump to specific topics if you have urgent questions. All content remains available for reference.

Implementation Timeline

You can start implementing strategies immediately, even while still watching lessons. Many parents begin with environment setup and routines, then move to settling techniques when ready.

Reference Back Anytime

All course content remains accessible on demand. Reference specific lessons, download resources again, or review modules as your baby grows and changes.

Is This Course Right for You?

This course is designed for parents of babies between 3-4 months old who are experiencing sleep challenges or want to prepare for upcoming changes.

  • Best for: Babies aged 3-4 months (or approaching this age) experiencing cat naps, frequent night wakings, or parents wanting to build better sleep foundations.
  • Time commitment: Self-paced learning. Estimated 3-4 hours to complete all content, but can be spread over days or weeks.
  • What you'll need: Willingness to implement strategies, time to complete checklists, and patience as changes take time to establish.
  • Not the right fit? If your baby is outside the 3-4 month window, check out our Newborn Sleep Guide or 5-12 Month Guide instead.
