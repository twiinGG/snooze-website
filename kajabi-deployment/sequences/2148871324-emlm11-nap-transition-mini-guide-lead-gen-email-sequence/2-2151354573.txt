Hi {{ first_name }},
If you have started the transition, this is usually the week it looks like a mistake. Early morning waking, a shorter second nap, and a baby who falls apart before dinner.
That is the transition, not a sign you got it wrong. Two naps have to stretch across a day that three naps used to cover, and the gap shows up at the ends first.
The fix is almost always an earlier bedtime while they adjust, not a longer day to tire them out. Push the day and the early waking gets worse.
