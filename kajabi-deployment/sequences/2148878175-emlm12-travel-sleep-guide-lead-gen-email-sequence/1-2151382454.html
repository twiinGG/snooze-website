Start with the first section. The single most useful thing you can do before you travel is focus on a really solid week of sleep leading into your trip, because well-rested babies cope much better with disruption.

One thing before you start planning: if you are heading into a new time zone, do not try to shift your baby onto it before you leave. Prioritise great sleep at home and then crack on with the new time zone once you get there.
