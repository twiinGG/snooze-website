The thing that catches almost every new parent out is that an overtired newborn does not look tired. They look wired. Wide eyes, arching, fighting the cot, feeding in short bursts and unsettling the moment you put them down.

So the natural read is that they are not ready for sleep, and the awake window stretches further, and the next sleep is worse than the last one.

Watching the clock rather than the baby is what breaks that loop at this age. Not rigidly, just closely enough to catch them before the window closes.
